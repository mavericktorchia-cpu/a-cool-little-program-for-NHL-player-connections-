    How to play
    • You start with one clue. Guess any time.
    • A wrong guess, or Next clue, unlocks the next clue. There are 7.
    • Each wrong guess shows what it has in common with the mystery player: position, debut decade, teams, and career length.
    • After all 7 clues, you get one last guess.
    • Everyone gets the same daily player. A new one starts at midnight, your time.
