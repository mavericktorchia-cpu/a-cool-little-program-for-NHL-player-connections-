    How to play
    • Type a player who was on the same team, in the same season, as the last player in your chain.
    • When you add someone who played with the target, the chain closes and you're done.
    • Par is the shortest possible chain. Match it for a perfect score.
    • Wrong names count as misses. Undo is free. A hint first shows a team and season to look at, then the next player's initials.
    • Everyone gets the same daily puzzle. A new one starts at midnight, your time.
