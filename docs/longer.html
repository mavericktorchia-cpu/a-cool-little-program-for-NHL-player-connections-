How to play
  • Seasons counts every season a player was on an NHL roster. Teams counts different team names, so a franchise that moved (Quebec to Colorado) counts twice. Teammates counts everyone they shared a roster with.
  • Daily 10: ten rounds, the same for everyone today, mixing all three stats. Share your score.
  • Endless: the winner stays on and you keep going until you miss. Pairs get closer as your streak grows.
  • Ties never come up: every pair has a clear winner.
Stats come from season-by-season NHL rosters in the public NHL API. Fan project, not affiliated with or endorsed by the National Hockey League. Source on GitHub
