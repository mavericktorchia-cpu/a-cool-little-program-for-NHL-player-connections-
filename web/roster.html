Name the Roster

You get a team and a season. Name as many players from that roster as you can before the clock runs out.

Named0/0
Time3:00

How to play
  • Type a player's name. A last name is enough, and a correct name fills in as soon as you finish typing it.
  • If two players on the roster share a last name, type the first name too.
  • The roster is everyone who was on the team at any point that season, traded players included.
  • A wrong name tells you if that player was on the team in a different season.
  • Everyone gets the same daily roster. A new one starts at midnight, your time.
